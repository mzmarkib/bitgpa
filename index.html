FAQ
Deals with a few questions that frequently arise among students and are not always answered. If you have any further questions you can ask them via the details in the contact page. We hope they are helpful...
How many times can I repeat a subject?
You can have up to Three (3) attempts on a particular subject.
What is the highest grade that can I get when I attempt a particular subject for the second time?
You can obtain any grade up to an A, but when accounting the Grade Point Value (GPV) for that particular subject can be up to 2.00 (GPV of a C grade).
What is the Difference between an A grade and an A+ grade?
Both A and A+ grades carry the same Grade Point Value (GPV) of 4.00, but when calculating GPA for class awarding, an A+ will be given a Grade Point Value (GPV) of 4.25.
If I applied for a particular Exam and did not attend the exam, will it still count as an Attempt?
No, if you are absent for an exam it will be counted as NOT SAT. On the other hand, you are expected to produce a medical certificate for your absence.
What is a Credit?
It is the measure of how much content a particular subject carries. A credit is equal to 15 hours of lectures or 30-45 hours of practical or an equivalent combination of lectures.

Award of Honours and Classes

A student qualifying for the award of a general degree (3 year) by completing all the relevant requirements shall also be qualified for the award of a class as follows.

(a). First Class for obtaining a minimum overall GPA of 3.50

(b). Second Class (Upper Division) – for obtaining a minimum overall GPA of 3.25

(c). Second Class (Lower Division) – for obtaining a minimum overall GPA of 3.00
